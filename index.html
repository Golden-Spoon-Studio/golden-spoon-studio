ABOUT

Hey! I’m Braydon, the solo developer behind Golden Spoon Studio. After spending years working as a chef, I eventually made the jump into software development — and somehow game development became the thing that let me combine both worlds. I build all of this outside of my full-time job, usually late at night with way too much coffee involved. Any support genuinely helps more than I can say. It goes toward art, music, software, assets, and most importantly gives me more time to keep making games. Even sharing the project, wish listing, or following along makes a huge difference for a small indie developer like me.

Thanks for stopping by, and feel free to reach out with any questions, feedback, or just to say hello.
