ABOUT

Hey! I’m Braydon, the solo developer behind Golden Spoon Studio. After years working as a chef, I transitioned into software development and combined both passions to create quirky, retro-inspired games with a culinary twist. My goal is to make fun, memorable experiences that feel creative, charming, and approachable for everyone.

Thanks for stopping by, and feel free to reach out with any questions, feedback, or just to say hello.

CHEESECAKE FROGS - COMING FALL 2026

Escape the sewers and bounce your way back to the surface in this quirky retro-inspired adventure. Eat magical homemade cheesecakes to float through dangerous areas, avoid sharp hazards, and try not to pop on the way up.
